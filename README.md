## Featured Projects

### 🔹 Blinkit Sales & Outlet Performance Analysis
**[View Repository →](https://github.com/KS-Sonu/blinkit-sales-analysis)**

> *One outlet. 41 years. 10× the competition.*

End-to-end business diagnosis of 8,500+ item-level sales records across 10 Blinkit outlets. Five insights found through hypothesis testing — three hypotheses were rejected by data before the real answers emerged.

**Key findings:**
- Supermarket Type3 generates ₹3,694 AOV — 60% above Type1 and 10× above Grocery Stores — driven by 41-year customer trust and basket depth, not pricing or visibility
- Grocery Store's low AOV is a format reality, not underperformance — it serves a different customer mission entirely
- Tier 3's sales lead is a composition effect — outlet type mix, not customer behaviour
- Premium item share does not explain AOV — basket depth does
- Sales Efficiency Ratio reveals Household as top shelf performer; Breakfast underconverts its space

**Tools:** Power BI · Excel · Python · SQL
